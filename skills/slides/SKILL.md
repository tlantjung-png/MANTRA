# Slides

## Use When

Use for presentations, proposals, pitches, teaching decks, or speaker-note deliverables.

## Procedure

1. Clarify audience, purpose, length, decision, and native terminology.
2. Outline one message per slide with a clear story arc.
3. Build consistent layouts, visual hierarchy, charts, and speaker notes.
4. Check overflow, readability, alignment, and transitions.
5. Inspect the deck as an audience member and revise weak or overloaded slides.

## Verification

Reopen the artifact and confirm slide count, notes, shapes, text, layout, and absence of clipping.

## Boundaries

Do not create a raw data dump, hide essential context in notes, or invent metrics and claims.
