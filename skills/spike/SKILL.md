# Spike

## Use When

Use for prototypes, feasibility questions, approach comparisons, or uncertain integrations.

## Procedure

1. Decompose uncertainty into risk-ranked questions and define what would count as success.
2. Research existing options and project constraints.
3. Build the smallest isolated experiment needed to answer the questions.
4. Exercise edge cases and record surprises, limitations, and environmental assumptions.
5. Decide whether the idea is validated, partially validated, or invalidated; recommend the next step.

## Verification

The spike record must identify evidence, scope, observed behavior, and why the verdict follows.

## Boundaries

Keep spikes disposable and out of production paths. Do not use a spike to avoid a known answer or carry experimental code forward without a separate implementation decision.
